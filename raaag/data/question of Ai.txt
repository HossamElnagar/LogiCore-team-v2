🟡 2. Delivery Dispute

Q:

What should I do if a customer says they didn't receive their shipment?

Expected Answer:

Verify the delivery signature or photo proof. If the proof is missing or unclear, initiate a trace with the driver. If the customer is still not satisfied, offer a partial refund or re-delivery.

Q:

What happens if delivery proof is missing?

Expected Answer:

Initiate a trace with the driver.

Q:

How long do we have to resolve a delivery dispute?

Expected Answer:

All delivery disputes must be resolved within 48 hours.

🔵 3. Vehicle Breakdown

Q:

What should a driver do if the delivery vehicle breaks down?

Expected Answer:

The driver should park safely, turn on the hazard lights, and contact the dispatch center. Dispatch will arrange a replacement vehicle or tow truck. The driver must stay with the vehicle and ensure the cargo is secure.

Q:

Who arranges a replacement vehicle after a breakdown?

Expected Answer:

The dispatch center arranges a replacement vehicle or tow truck.

Q:

How long is the estimated resolution time for a vehicle breakdown?

Expected Answer:

The estimated resolution time is 2–4 hours.

🟠 4. Delayed Shipment

Q:

What happens when a shipment is delayed?

Expected Answer:

The system automatically sends an SMS and email to the customer every 2 hours until delivery.

Q:

When should customer service call a customer about a delay?

Expected Answer:

The customer service team should proactively call the customer if the delay exceeds 4 hours.

Q:

What compensation should we offer for a delayed shipment?

Expected Answer:

Offer a discount voucher for the customer's next shipment.

🟣 5. Return Policy

Q:

What happens when a customer returns an item?

Expected Answer:

The item is inspected for quality and restocked if it is in sellable condition. Defective items are sent to the claims department.

Q:

How long does a customer have to wait for a refund?

Expected Answer:

The customer is refunded within 3 business days.

Q:

What reason codes can be used for returns?

Expected Answer:

Returns can be logged with reasons such as damaged, wrong item, or customer changed mind.
